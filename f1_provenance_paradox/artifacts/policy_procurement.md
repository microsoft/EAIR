# AcmeCorp Procurement Policy PR-12

**Owner:** Procurement Governance  
**Applies to:** Third-party purchases and renewals above USD 25,000  
**Review cycle:** Annual

## Purpose and Scope

This policy establishes minimum commercial controls for supplier selection, contracting, renewal, and exit. Business owners remain accountable for service outcomes, while Procurement confirms competitive process, total cost, and contractual exit flexibility. A purchase order does not replace required legal, privacy, security, or finance review.

## Sourcing and Approval

Purchases from USD 25,000 through USD 100,000 require two documented market comparisons unless Procurement approves a sole-source rationale. Purchases above USD 100,000 normally require three comparisons and approval from the relevant cost-center vice president. Multi-year commitments must state the full committed value in the approval record. Implementation fees, usage charges, and expected renewal increases are included when calculating value.

The business owner must document the operational need, intended users, data categories, key dependencies, and an accountable service owner before signature. Procurement may accept an existing group framework agreement where it offers equivalent or stronger protections.

## Renewal and Exit Controls

Supplier agreements must preserve a practical opportunity to change providers, renegotiate, or discontinue service. An automatic-renewal clause is permitted only when the notice period for non-renewal is no more than 60 calendar days before the end of the current term. Any longer exit notice period requires a written exception approved by the Director of Procurement before signature. A supplier reminder notice does not cure a non-compliant contractual notice period.

Business owners must record renewal dates in the contract repository within ten business days after signature. Procurement Operations sends reminders 120, 90, and 60 days before renewal, but those reminders are an administrative control and do not shift accountability from the owner.

## Records and Supplier Management

The sourcing record must retain bids, approvals, conflict disclosures, the executed agreement, and material negotiation notes for seven years. Supplier performance for critical services is reviewed at least annually. Reviews should consider availability, support quality, security issues, invoice accuracy, concentration risk, and progress against remediation commitments.

Gifts or hospitality that could influence a sourcing decision must be declined and reported under the Business Conduct Policy. Procurement personnel must disclose personal or financial relationships with a bidder. Violations may result in suspension of purchasing authority and referral to Compliance.
