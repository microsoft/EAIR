# AcmeCorp Contracting Standard CS-4

**Owner:** Office of the General Counsel  
**Applies to:** Supplier agreements for software and business services  
**Classification:** Internal

## Contract Formation

Contracts must identify the legal entities, scope, fees, term, governing documents, and authorized signatories. Order forms should not silently override a master agreement. Material overrides must cite the affected provision. Click-through terms may be used only for low-risk purchases within delegated authority and may not govern regulated data or business-critical services.

Statements of work must include deliverables, acceptance criteria, dependencies, change control, and ownership of work product. Estimates should be distinguished from fixed commitments. Supplier marketing claims and roadmap statements are not contractual commitments unless incorporated in writing.

## Liability Baseline

For paid software and hosted services, the supplier's aggregate liability cap must be no lower than fees paid or payable for the 12 months preceding the event giving rise to a claim. A cap based on fewer than 12 months of fees requires written approval from an AcmeCorp Legal Director and a documented assessment of service criticality, data exposure, available insurance, and replacement cost.

The baseline cap is not automatically sufficient for every engagement. Legal may require a higher cap or a separate enhanced cap for confidentiality breaches, security incidents, data protection claims, intellectual property indemnity, gross negligence, or willful misconduct. AcmeCorp payment obligations may be excluded from the mutual cap only when remedies for disputed invoices remain available.

## Warranties and Indemnities

Software suppliers should warrant material conformity with documentation, professional performance of services, and compliance with applicable law. Warranty remedies must not prevent AcmeCorp from using termination or damages remedies for persistent material failure. Intellectual property indemnity should include defense and settlement obligations and a practical remedy if use is enjoined.

AcmeCorp does not provide broad indemnities for ordinary use of supplier services. Any customer indemnity must be tied to AcmeCorp-provided content or a proven breach of the agreement and must follow notice, control-of-defense, and consent-to-settlement safeguards.

## Governance

Contract owners must store the signed contract and approvals in the repository. Deviations from this standard require an exception that identifies the provision, business rationale, risk owner, compensating controls, approver, and expiration date. Commercial urgency alone is not an approval basis. Legal may publish clause guidance, but current policy and the executed text control.
