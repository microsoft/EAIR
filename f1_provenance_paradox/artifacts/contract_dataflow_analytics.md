## 1. Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 **Affiliate** means an entity that directly or indirectly controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with a party. **Authorized User** means an employee or contractor whom Customer authorizes to use the Services for Customer's internal business purposes. **Customer Data** means electronic data submitted to the Services by or for Customer, excluding usage telemetry that has been aggregated so that it does not identify Customer or any individual. **Documentation** means Vendor's then-current user guides and technical materials. **Order Form** means an ordering document executed by both parties and referencing this Agreement. **Services** means the hosted analytics, reporting, connector, and support services described in an Order Form.

1.2 References to writing include email unless a provision expressly requires a signed notice. Headings are for convenience and do not affect interpretation. The words "including" and "include" mean including without limitation. If an Order Form conflicts with this Agreement, the Order Form controls only where it identifies the conflicting provision and states the parties' intent to override it. The English-language version controls over any translation.

1.3 A business day is a day other than Saturday, Sunday, or a public holiday in the place where notice is received. Time periods measured in days are calendar days unless stated otherwise. Obligations that by their nature should continue after expiration, including confidentiality, payment, intellectual property, warranty disclaimers, and limitations of liability, survive.

## 2. Subscription Services

Customer may permit its Affiliates to use the Services under Customer's account, provided Customer remains responsible for their compliance with this Agreement.

2.2 Vendor will make the Services available in material accordance with the Documentation and the service levels in Section 5. Vendor may improve or modify features during the Subscription Term, provided a modification does not materially reduce the core analytics and reporting functionality purchased by Customer. Vendor will provide reasonable advance notice of a material feature retirement and, where commercially practicable, identify an alternative workflow.

Vendor may use telemetry to operate, secure, support, and improve the Services and to create aggregated benchmarking data. Vendor will not publish benchmarking data in a manner that identifies Customer. Telemetry containing personal data remains subject to Section 9.

2.4 The Services are not designed to make autonomous employment, credit, medical, or other legally significant decisions. Customer is responsible for human review of reports and recommendations before using them in a decision affecting an individual. Vendor does not warrant that statistical output will be suitable for every Customer use case.

## 5. Service Levels and Support

5.1 Vendor targets monthly production availability of 99.9%, excluding scheduled maintenance announced at least five business days in advance, emergency maintenance necessary to protect the Services, and downtime caused by Customer systems, unsupported integrations, force majeure, or internet infrastructure outside Vendor's control.

5.2 If measured availability falls below 99.9% in a calendar month, Customer may request a service credit equal to five percent of the affected monthly fee. If availability falls below 99.0%, the credit is ten percent; below 95.0%, the credit is twenty-five percent. Requests must be submitted within thirty days after the affected month and include reasonable supporting details. Credits are applied to a future invoice and are Customer's sole monetary remedy for availability failure.

5.3 Vendor provides email and portal support during published business hours. Severity 1 incidents, meaning production service is unavailable for most users with no reasonable workaround, receive a target initial response within one hour. Severity 2 incidents receive a target response within four business hours. Response targets are not resolution commitments.

5.4 Vendor maintains business continuity and disaster recovery procedures appropriate to the Services and tests material elements at least annually. On request, Vendor will provide an executive summary of the latest test, subject to confidentiality restrictions and reasonable redaction of security-sensitive details.

## 6. Security Program

6.1 Vendor will maintain an information security program with administrative, technical, and physical safeguards reasonably designed to protect Customer Data against accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure, or access. The program includes access controls, security training, vulnerability management, logging, incident response, and risk-based review of service providers.

6.2 Vendor will encrypt Customer Data in transit over public networks using current industry-standard protocols and at rest in production storage using generally accepted encryption methods. Access to production Customer Data will be limited to personnel and service accounts with a business need, subject to authentication and logging controls.

6.3 Vendor will notify Customer without undue delay after confirming a security incident that compromises the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of Customer Data. Notices will include information reasonably available about the nature of the incident, likely consequences, measures taken, and a contact for follow-up. Vendor may provide information in phases and will not characterize an event as legally reportable on Customer's behalf.

6.4 Vendor will complete an independent security controls assessment at least annually. On written request no more than once per year, Vendor will provide its then-current SOC 2 Type II report or a comparable summary under confidentiality. Customer may submit reasonable written security questions when the report does not address a material control relevant to the Services.

Vendor will process such data to provide, secure, and support the Services, comply with documented instructions, and meet legal obligations.

9.2 Vendor will ensure personnel authorized to process Customer Data are subject to confidentiality obligations. Vendor will reasonably assist Customer with data subject requests, security assessments, and legally required impact assessments, considering the nature of processing and information available to Vendor. Customer is responsible for determining whether the Services and its instructions meet Customer's regulatory obligations.

9.3 Customer authorizes Vendor to engage any subprocessor in any jurisdiction, subject to commercially reasonable safeguards appropriate to the processing. Such subprocessors may support hosting, customer service, security, analytics, and related service operations. Vendor will remain responsible for each subprocessor's performance of the data protection obligations Vendor assigns to it and will maintain a current list of material subprocessors through its trust portal.

9.4 Vendor will provide at least fifteen days' advance notice through the trust portal before a new material subprocessor begins processing Customer Data. Customer may raise a reasonable, documented data protection objection during that period. The parties will discuss a commercially reasonable solution. If no solution is available, Customer may discontinue the affected feature, and Vendor will refund prepaid fees allocable to the unused portion of that feature.

9.5 On expiration or termination, Vendor will make Customer Data available for export for thirty days through standard functionality and then delete it from active systems according to its retention schedule, unless law requires retention. Backup copies will be isolated from ordinary use and deleted through normal rotation. Vendor may retain aggregated data that does not identify Customer or an individual.

11.1 This Agreement begins on the Effective Date and continues while an Order Form remains active. The initial Subscription Term is twelve months. Either party may terminate for the other's uncured material breach after thirty days' written notice, or immediately if the other becomes insolvent, ceases business, or enters a comparable proceeding not dismissed within sixty days.

11.2 At the end of the initial Subscription Term and each renewal term, the subscription automatically renews for an additional twelve-month period unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least ninety days before the current term ends. Vendor may increase renewal fees by up to five percent by giving notice at least one hundred twenty days before renewal; a larger increase requires mutual agreement.

11.3 On termination for Vendor's uncured material breach, Vendor will refund prepaid subscription fees allocable to the period after termination. On termination for Customer's breach, unpaid committed fees become due to the extent permitted by law. Termination does not affect accrued rights or remedies.

11.4 Vendor may terminate a discontinued free beta feature on reasonable notice without terminating paid Services. Customer may stop using an optional feature at any time, but this does not reduce committed subscription fees unless an Order Form expressly provides otherwise.
